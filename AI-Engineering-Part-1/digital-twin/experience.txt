Data Analyst Consultant, Citizens' Ambulance Service (Indiana, PA), September 2026. Nangi worked as an independent contractor for Citizens' Ambulance Service, a nonprofit EMS provider founded in 1964. It serves Indiana County and parts of Armstrong, Westmoreland and Clearfield Counties, and answers more than 8,400 emergency calls a year. 

Nangi was brought in to audit and modernize the organization's operational and financial reporting, centered on a legacy Excel workbook inherited from a predecessor. 

Nangi mapped what the workbook tracked, who relied on its outputs (board, billing and state reporting) and how it was maintained, and checked it for data-sensitivity risks such as patient or billing information. 

Nangi used an audit-before-changing approach and recommended running the old and new systems side by side, so a single fragile workbook wouldn't be the organization's only point of failure. 

Nangi also proposed concrete Excel improvements: structured Tables, named ranges, Power Query, data validation, protected cells, and documented helper columns in place of deeply nested formulas. Alongside that, Nangi assessed when it would make sense to move beyond Excel to a SQL and Power BI setup.
