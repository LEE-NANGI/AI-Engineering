Project Falcon — kickoff notes
- Estimated budget: $42,000
- Launch target: end of Q3
- Owner: Priya
- Core team: Marco (engineering), Lena (design), Sam (go-to-market)
What it is:
Falcon is a tech news and discussion community with AI avatars who provide commentary
It's like a Hacker News + a Late Night Talk Show, but in written format and with AI commentators.
The bet: engaging AI avatars will quickly become fan-favourites because of the unique dynamic between them.
Premise:
Ship before the competition announces theirs; quiet until then
Hacker News is the benchmark — we study it constantly to understand what the community wants
Two competitors rumored to be building something similar — speed is the moat
'Falcon' is a placeholder; marketing hates it, but it has stuck
Priya's one hard rule: do not slip the Q3 date to add features
Open question: what are the most popular categories on Hacker News?
